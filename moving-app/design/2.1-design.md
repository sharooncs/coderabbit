# HouseMove 2.1: first run, several moves, one app per role

Final design after four audit rounds (three independent reviewers plus my own pass). Nothing here is built yet.

## 1. The flows

### First open
Two large buttons: **Start a new move** and **Join a move**. Under Join, a small link: **Lost your phone?**

### Start a new move (you become its Organiser)
1. **What is it for:** four picture tiles. A line: "Has someone already started this move? Join theirs instead."
2. **The move:** name filled in, date optional, your name optional (say or type it).
3. **Rooms in the old home:** tap tiles, or skip.
4. **Rooms in the new home:** "Same as old home" in one tap, or skip (left out for "just listing").
5. The move is saved, then optional **Who's helping?**:
   - "Add a second Organiser, so the move never depends on one phone."
   - Add people: say or type a name, tap a role, repeat.
   - Organisers and Deciders get their own invite. Helpers can all use one **Helper code** (10 minutes; each phone gives the name it already knows).
   - Invites show one at a time. For an Organiser or Decider, the joining phone first asks "Join Maple Road as Priya, Decider?" (Yes / Not me), then the Organiser sees "Joined: Priya, on Priya's phone" with Right / Undo before anything is sent. Helpers who joined appear in a list, each with Remove.
   - **Do this later** goes to Home. People opens this screen any time.
   - Moves with one person (just listing, storage on your own) show no people, approvals or invites until a second person is added.

### Join a move
1. The camera opens at once.
2. "Join **Maple Road** as **Priya, Decider**?" Yes / Not me. Nothing is sent before Yes.
3. If this phone has a move of its own with things in it and nobody else: "Bring your 23 things into Maple Road?" (default No; never offered for just-listing or storage moves; the old move is archived, not deleted).
4. A banner on Home says who you are now and what your role does.
- Phones need to be on the same Wi-Fi, or one phone turns on its own hotspot (Android shows a Wi-Fi QR, so nobody types a password).
- No app yet? The Organiser taps **Share the app** (Quick Share, works without internet), or shows a download QR when there is internet.

### Every later open
The move used last opens. Its name at the top shows your role ("Maple Road · Helper") and a dot when another move on the phone needs you. Tap it for **Your moves**: a card per move (name, your role, days to go, progress, last synced), start or join another, and per card back up, archive or remove. Finished moves fold under "Earlier moves".

## 2. One app, shaped by role
- Same dock for everyone: Home, Items, Boxes, Move day, More. Scan and Find sit at the top of every screen.
- **Organiser's Home:** approvals waiting, people, the six phases.
- **Decider's Home:** items waiting for a decision, what happened to their suggestions, costs to suggest.
- **Helper's Home:** what is physically next for the things (pack, load, unload, unpack, sweep, meter photos), driven by where things are rather than the date; jobs given to them today; Ask a question.
- **Everyone:** Sync on Home; a banner when a role changes.
- **Helpers can:** pack, scan, load, unload, unpack, sweep, take meter and condition photos, report damage, find things, ask for a decision, tick jobs given to them, and mark an approved sale or donation **handed over**. Every Helper sees "Paid" or "Not paid" on it; only the Helper doing the hand-over sees the amount.
- **Helpers are not sent:** costs and cost suggestions, receipts, prices and sale amounts, item values, serial numbers, make and model, warranty, serial-label photos. Find leaves out what is marked "travels with me". Helpers cannot make backups. Disassembly and cable photos reach them at full size.
- **Role changes:** a person who becomes a Helper loses the hidden data from their phone after their next full sync with an Organiser or Decider. A promoted person sees "Costs and decisions arrive at your next sync with an Organiser or Decider" until then. An Organiser confirms before stepping down; the last Organiser cannot.
- **Printed:** the full sheet for Organisers; a half-page Helper card that also works without the app (door-sign colours; "write KIT on two sides").

## 3. Moves, phones and labels
- **Move ID:** made once by the founding phone, never changed, checked before any data moves (sync, invite, backup, update). Moves from 2.0 get one worked out from their founder; if two phones work out different ones, they fall back once to 2.0's same-move check and keep both as aliases. The data-format version goes up, so a 2.0 phone gets "different versions" (with Share the app) instead of mixing.
- **One card per move ID:** joining, restoring or opening an update for a move already on the phone goes into that card.
- **Phone ID:** each installed copy has its own, kept out of Android's backup. Adding a device never retires another. Retiring is explicit ("Retire Sam's old phone"); a retired phone is accepted once, one way: its work comes in, nothing goes back.
- **Letters and label numbers:** handed out from blocks owned by the admitting phone, kept beside the phone ID, never reused, and unique across the moves on one phone. New labels carry a short move tag; an old label found in two moves asks which.
- **Per move:** person, letter, code counter, current room and trip, last sync and backup, photos, label range. **Per phone:** phone ID, your name, book-lookup choice.

## 4. Working apart
- Everyone joins once in person.
- **Send update:** through any messaging app, encrypted to every Organiser and Decider phone the sender knows; only what the receiver's role may see; opened with the "Open update" button on the move's card and merged with the same rules as a sync.
- **Ask for decisions:** for family who never visit. Sends a picture list of undecided items through any messaging app; the Organiser taps the answers into Triage.

## 5. Safety nets
- **Daily small backup** on any move with a single Organiser (one-person moves included): no ordinary photos, but receipt and serial-label photos made smaller. Saved every day to a place picked once (a Drive folder, Documents, a USB stick). Home nudges when the last is a week old, and asks again for a second Organiser a few days before move day. Android's own backup holds only this small data.
- **Restore** makes the phone the person whose phone made the backup (a Helper if that person is gone); a phone already in the move merges and stays itself.
- **Lost your phone?** restores the daily backup or a backup file; with another Organiser, they accept the new phone.
- **Delete a move:** only while nobody else has ever joined. **Remove from this phone:** first lists what no other phone has and offers Sync, Send update or Back up; the last Organiser cannot remove a shared move.
- **Helpers' copies expire** a few days after the move is marked done, once an Organiser's or Decider's phone has all their work (syncing only among Helpers does not count). "Done, thanks Sam" does it at once on the same condition.
- **Removed from a move:** the card offers to send unsynced work to an Organiser as an update, then removes itself.
- **A change refused at a merge** becomes an item on Organisers' Home, without showing cost details to anyone not allowed them.

## 6. Merges keep progress
- Stage, trip and place (old home, van, storage, new home) merge together as one stamped part, so undo and "back from storage" work.
- Damage reports are separate records, each cleared on its own.
- Contents lines are separate records with deletions: a removed line never comes back, nothing is counted twice.
- Price and sale have their own stamped part in the hidden record; at upgrade the old values are checked and re-sealed with the same author and time, so no decision or sale resets. Helpers never write the hidden record (a serial from a Helper comes in as a photo or a question).

## 7. Storage, minimal
One "Storage unit" place with no rooms; trips can go there and things there count as in storage, not delivered; a later "Bring back from storage" trip goes to the new home.

## 8. Fix now in 2.0.1 (two small checks, no data-format change)
1. **Restore:** first, if the phone's own person is in the backup, merge and stay itself. Otherwise, on a phone that is alone, leave its own move behind the way joining does, then become the person whose phone made the backup, or a Helper if that person is missing or removed. When merging into a shared move, unknown people whose only grants are open are not taken in.
2. **Join:** when the phone is already in a shared move (not alone), it joins only if the host is a known member whose role is Organiser; otherwise: "This phone is already in Elm Street move, and HouseMove 2.0 holds one move per phone. If this is the same move, sync with an Organiser first." A phone that is alone joins as today.

## 9. Known limits
- Someone who can never meet in person cannot join until there is an online service; "Ask for decisions" covers their decisions meanwhile.
- An app deliberately modified to forge records is not caught; there are no signatures.
- Backups already saved elsewhere cannot be recalled.
- Android now requires apps to come from a verified developer in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore and Thailand (since 30 September 2026) and worldwide from 2027; the signing key should be registered with Google before then.
